# Google Play listing assets for 4.4

The screenshots in `pt-BR/` and `en-US/` were captured from the app on an Android emulator using a synthetic demonstration ride around Parque Ibirapuera. The coordinates and activity are fabricated for the listing preview; they do not come from the private GPX validation fixtures.

Each locale includes the live activity screen, saved activities, activity summary, elevation metrics, route map, and settings. The three GPX validation activities do not contain heart-rate samples, so these screenshots do not show heart-rate values.
